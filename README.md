## Wireless Mouse Buying Guide

A wireless mouse is a useful accessory for students, professionals, and everyday computer users. Before purchasing one, consider these factors:

### 1. Connectivity
Check whether the mouse uses a USB wireless receiver or Bluetooth. Choose a connection that works with your device.

### 2. Comfort
Look for a comfortable shape and size, especially if you use your computer for long hours.

### 3. Battery Life
Compare battery performance and check whether the mouse uses replaceable or rechargeable batteries.

### 4. Tracking Performance
For regular browsing and office work, smooth and consistent tracking is important.

### 5. Build Quality
Check the buttons, scroll wheel, materials, and customer feedback before purchasing.
